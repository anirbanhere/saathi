# Offline speech tariff arithmetic

`estimateSttCost` in `openbot/server/src/self-hosted/stt-cost.ts` is a pure internal calculator, imported only by its tests. It does not register a provider, read configuration/keys, access media/storage/network, reserve funds, or grant consent/admission/execution. Existing fixture-only claims and all default/public flags stay unchanged. This is the first arithmetic increment of `tasks/live-stt-admission-design.md`, not a live admission policy.

Callers supply a trusted reviewed tariff, trusted inspected/rounded duration, a per-request monetary ceiling and an explicit clock. Missing/nonliteral enablement denies by default. Strict schemas reject unknown fields, unsafe integers, invalid billing intervals and malformed clocks; no currency conversion or input coercion occurs. Supported currency labels are INR/USD, each expressed in integer millionths of that currency. Provider/model/revision fields identify the caller's price snapshot; syntax acceptance does not verify catalogue availability or authoritative pricing.

The formula rounds the greater of duration/minimum up to a billing quantum, then rounds the rational micro-unit charge up and adds the explicit fixed fee. BigInt arithmetic preserves intermediate precision; an unrepresentable final integer denies. The exact ceiling is inclusive, and expired/future tariffs or mismatched currency deny. Results are frozen scalar estimates or fixed-code denials, with no private token, approval or lease. In-process getter errors are sanitized. Example tests use entirely synthetic rates, never a published vendor price.

The helper cannot verify account tier/balance/credits, tax/fees, consent/media authenticity, owner/global attempt/audio/money quotas, concurrent reservations, remote idempotency, cancellation billing or actual cost. Those remain required future boundaries. A new executor must atomically reserve durable budgets, pin reviewed complete pricing evidence, revalidate current consent, authenticate one-shot claims and reconcile unknown external outcomes before any separately authorized provider call. Never use a successful estimate alone to invoke a vendor or promise a monetary spending guarantee.

Tests cover upward quantum/minimum/rational rounding, fee/zero rate, exact ceiling, currencies, stale/unknown tariff, malformed/nonfinite/unsafe values, large exact arithmetic/overflow, metadata override denial, scalar pins/immutability and sanitized input. Existing profiles and production data are untouched. Exact verification and draft PR evidence belong in HANDOFF.md.
